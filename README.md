## In this repository

### Facebook Ads Campaign Analysis (Python)
[Open the notebook](Viktoriia_Korzh_Facebook_Ads_Analysis.ipynb)

Analysis of 11 Facebook Ads campaigns (Nov 2020 to Nov 2022, $195.8K spend) in Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn:
daily spend and ROMI, campaign comparison, distributions, and correlations.

**Key finding:** Expansion and Lookalike take 67% of the budget with ROMI around 1.25,
while Promos and Trendy deliver ROMI of 1.76 and 1.91 on just 3.6% of spend.
Their worst days are still profitable, which makes them the first candidates for a scaling test.
